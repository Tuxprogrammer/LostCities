# 0.1.1-beta

City shape and ocean fixes. New worlds only: a world pins its profile when created, so existing worlds keep their old city layout.

## Changed
- **Cities connect.** A city chunk must join its city's center edge to edge, so there are no stray one-chunk cities (`cityConnected`).
- **Flatter cities.** Ground levels stay within `cityGrade` levels of the city's base and follow the mean height of the surrounding `cityGradeRadius` chunks, so streets and buildings no longer sit 10 to 20 blocks apart.
- **No cities on the sea floor.** Ocean-biome chunks and chunks whose terrain is under sea level are never city (`cityAvoidOcean`).
- **`rarecities` retuned:** about twice the distance between cities and about four times the area (`cityChance` 0.00025, radius 100 to 256).
- New profile options: `cityGrade`, `cityGradeRadius`, `cityAvoidOcean`, `cityConnected`.

## Fixed
- Thin sand or stone walls enclosing a pool of sea near cities. The terrain blend raised sea-floor columns toward the city's height; it now leaves sea floor alone.

## Developer
- `-Dlostcitiesnh.debugWool=true` writes every solid block the mod places as wool, colored per terrain stage, to show what the mod built.

## Known issues
- Highways and railways still plan against the unfiltered city mask, so one may end beside a chunk the new rules removed.
