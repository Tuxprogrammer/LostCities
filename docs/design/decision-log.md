# Decision log

<!-- One row per decision. Newest at the bottom. Larger decisions also get a numbered record in this folder; the log links it. A reversed decision is edited in place here and wherever it was applied. -->

Smaller decisions, made with the owner during planning or by the orchestrator during the build. Each row says what was decided and why.

| ID | Date | Area | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 2026-11-01 | Process | A change that meets the definition of done may be committed straight to `gtnh`. Parallel or long work happens on its own branch in a worktree. It is verified after merging `gtnh` in, merged with `--no-ff`, then the branch and worktree are removed. `gtnh` is pushed at roadmap gates and when the owner asks; `1.20` only tracks upstream | Owner's choice (TrainHorizons style); one revertible merge per parallel feature without a pull request per change |
| D2 | 2026-11-01 | Spec | The upstream behavior spec is area sheets plus a bug register: one sheet per system, with behavior, every number and tunable, asset formats and suspected bugs, and no per-entity pixel or UV detail ([original-spec/CONVENTIONS.md](../original-spec/CONVENTIONS.md)) | Owner's choice. Code is portable under [0001](0001-port-policy-and-licensing.md), so the spec is the test contract and the bug list, not a clean-room substitute for the code |
| D3 | 2026-11-01 | Tooling | `tools/semantic-search/` indexes this repository and every source and asset file on the local `1.20` branch, read from git without a checkout. Upstream hits are written `1.20:<path>` | Agents find upstream behavior by meaning before they know class names; one index serves every worktree |
| D4 | 2026-11-01 | Process | Builds, tests and Minecraft runs happen on the shared build server, never on a development laptop. Local Gradle, when unavoidable, runs one build at a time with `--no-daemon --max-workers=2 -Dorg.gradle.jvmargs=-Xmx2g`, and never `--stop`. Host details stay out of the repository | The development laptop has too little memory for Gradle daemons and Minecraft alongside the editor; the same rule holds in the owner's other mods |
| D5 | 2026-11-01 | Docs | [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) holds the binding rules (must and should). The skills in `.claude/skills/` hold the how-to. [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) is a thin map to both. The specs are `docs/original-spec/` (upstream) and `docs/new-spec/` (how this mod differs and fits the pack) | One home per kind of rule; the same split as the template the skills come from |
| D6 | 2026-11-01 | Code layout | Root package `com.tuxprogrammer.lostcities` with `asset` and `layout` as pure Java (no Minecraft types), `worldgen` as thin glue writing through a chunk-buffer interface, `client` for every client-only class, and `compat.<mod>` for optional integrations ([CONTRIBUTING § Architecture](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#architecture)) | World generation logic becomes unit-testable and deterministic by construction; static checks can enforce the boundaries by package |
| D7 | 2026-11-01 | Testing | World generation is tested in four layers: unit tests on layout and assets; golden-chunk hashes for fixed seeds, profiles and coordinates; headless worldgen scenarios on fresh worlds (no cascading generation, time budget, determinism across two worlds); and real-pack runs at the gates | Upstream relies on human visual checks only (its `AGENTS.md`); a backport across five Minecraft versions needs automated proof that layouts stay stable and correct |
| D8 | 2026-11-01 | Gates | The alpha gate's owner review is light: a screenshot set from the real pack, reviewed asynchronously. The interactive review (play sessions and back-and-forth on GTNH integrations and advanced features) happens in the beta phase ([ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md)) | Owner's choice; alpha proves the RWG overlay works, beta is where integration depth gets judged |
| D9 | 2026-11-01 | Determinism | Every upstream behavior that depends on generation order, timing or shared mutable caches is fixed, not ported: layout plans are immutable once built, edge blending never writes back into cached heights, and loot draws use their own seeded stream. This covers TERR-02, TERR-07, TERR-09, TERR-10, TERR-11, TERR-12, TERR-20, CITY-13, CITY-18, CITY-19, CITY-20, LOOT-05 and HWY-07 ([BUGS.md](../original-spec/BUGS.md)) | CONTRIBUTING's determinism rule; upstream's own multithreaded generation is where these come from, and a 1.7.10 port has no reason to keep them |
| D10 | 2026-11-01 | City layout | The world seed is part of every city-placement, radius, center-style and multibuilding seed, as it was on upstream `1.12` (fixes CITY-01, CITY-02, PROF-11). Each chunk of a city takes the city center's style instead of rolling its own (fixes CITY-03); the alpha screenshot review checks the look | Without the seed, every world has cities at the same coordinates; the per-chunk roll mixes styles inside one city |
| D11 | 2026-11-01 | Scope | Only upstream's default modes are ported: the intercity highway planner (legacy noise highways dropped), and `HIERARCHICAL_GRID_V1` streets (the `LEGACY` street mode dropped). The highway planner runs synchronously on the server thread and keeps no saved hub data: highways are a pure function of seed, coordinates and profile, with bounded caches (HWY-11 cannot occur) | Legacy modes exist for old 1.20 worlds, which a 1.7.10 port never loads; 1.7.10 generates on one thread; determinism rule |
| D12 | 2026-11-01 | Scope | Not ported before beta: the floating, space, cavern, spheres and cavernspheres landscape types, with the seven profiles that use them (`floating`, `space`, `cavern`, `biosphere`, `biosphere_caves`, `bio_wasteland`, `void_outside`) and the monorails only `space` generates. Their terrain comes from the Lost Worlds mod, which GTNH lacks, and [0003](0003-world-presence.md) targets the RWG overworld; they are the first candidates for the post-beta PersonalSpace work, where this mod controls the terrain, and the spec keeps their behavior on file. Not ported at all: the Lost City dimension and bed teleporter ([0003](0003-world-presence.md)); upstream's textures ([0001](0001-port-policy-and-licensing.md)); `src/api/.../DimensionDictionary.java` (a stale copy of a Recurrent Complex class, MIT at its source but not McJty's, which the upstream build doesn't compile and nothing references); and the ten config-only or dead profile options listed in [02-profiles-dimensions](../original-spec/02-profiles-dimensions/index.md) | Each is either unreachable in GTNH, not licensable by McJty (the textures), or unused |
| D13 | 2026-11-01 | Scope | The part editor and the file-writing commands are ported, operator-only, with every write confined to `config/lostcitiesnh/` (avoids CORE-28) | They are how new GTNH-themed buildings ([0004](0004-gtnh-layer-and-tier-ceiling.md)) get made |
| D14 | 2026-11-01 | Assets | Upstream's one-file-per-asset JSON layout is kept, loaded at `postInit` from the jar and then from `config/lostcitiesnh/assets/`, which overrides it. Converted assets keep upstream's 1.20 block names; one mapping table translates them to 1.7.10 blocks and metadata at load | Assets stay diffable against upstream for re-pins; the blocks with no 1.7.10 equivalent map in one place |
| D15 | 2026-11-01 | Assets | A child city style's selector list replaces its parent's when the child names that list, rather than appending to it (fixes ASSET-06) | Otherwise border cities keep the large multibuildings they ask to exclude, and repeated entries count twice |
| D16 | 2026-11-01 | Noise | The same-seed octaves of upstream's Perlin generator and the shared seed of the two legacy highway noise fields are kept as upstream has them (CORE-25 and HWY-03 recorded, not fixed); legacy highways are not ported anyway (D11) | The city distribution was tuned on this noise; changing it alters city density without fixing a visible defect |
| D17 | 2026-11-01 | Spec | The original spec paraphrases the 163 profile-option comments rather than quoting them, and records the 33 vanilla loot-table ids that `chestloot` names without their contents | The port writes its own lang keys, and the new spec replaces the loot with pack loot under the EV ceiling |
| D18 | 2026-11-01 | Ore veins | GT ore veins pass through cities: buildings are ore-inert (no block GT ore replaces; terracotta is the one block GT's boulders can replace, kept for upstream's look), nothing is excluded, and there is no restore pass at alpha. If the alpha screenshots show boulder damage, a restore pass limited to boulder-host blocks, through a mixin on GT's chunk task, fixes it ([ore-veins.md](../new-spec/00-pack-integration/ore-veins.md)) | Owner's choice; keeps all natural ore and no coupling to GT until it proves necessary |
| D19 | 2026-11-01 | Blocks | The block mapping in [block-mapping.md](../new-spec/00-pack-integration/block-mapping.md) is accepted; every infested (`monster_egg`) block maps to its plain block, so cities hold no silverfish. Visual choices are confirmed or redirected at the alpha screenshot review | Owner: silverfish are a pest not worth tearing down towers for, in buildings meant to be moved into |
| D20 | 2026-11-01 | Loot and mobs | City chests use this mod's own `ChestGenHooks` categories per tier band (Steam to EV) plus rail, filled at generation; vanilla categories only through an opt-in profile option; loot bags from tech, food, rail, farming and computers only. Spawners stay vanilla with vanilla entity names, Blaze included, at upstream's density ([loot-and-mobs.md](../new-spec/00-pack-integration/loot-and-mobs.md)) | The EV ceiling and the GTNH layer ([0004](0004-gtnh-layer-and-tier-ceiling.md)); faithful mobs because balance waits for beta ([beta balance list](../new-spec/beta-balance-list.md)) |
| D21 | 2026-11-01 | Other worldgen and spawn | Other mods' surface structures are built around, as [0006](0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) and D26 settle. Lakes, dungeons and the other population features are denied in city chunks or coexist as [other-worldgen.md](../new-spec/00-pack-integration/other-worldgen.md) lists. The pack's spawn is kept; upstream's spawn options are ported, off by default, and `forceSpawnInBuilding` applies only to a player's first join ([other-worldgen.md](../new-spec/00-pack-integration/other-worldgen.md), [spawn.md](../new-spec/00-pack-integration/spawn.md)) | Faithful defaults; the structure treatments follow the owner's coexistence rule (D24) |
| D22 | 2026-11-01 | Overlay | RWG builds its landscape first, terrain and decoration; the city is set into it, clearing RWG's decoration inside its footprint and keeping it outside ([0005](0005-rwg-overlay.md) §3a). Cities stay off worlds created before the mod unless the server owner opts in per world. Revisited after the reviews if cities feel too synthetic | Owner's choice: a lived-in look without trees on streets and roofs |
| D23 | 2026-11-01 | Licensing | The mod stays MIT by default. Relicensing to GPL-3.0 is pre-approved by the owner as a later option, taken only when a concrete integration needs it: porting or adapting code from a GPL pack mod such as RWG, RWG2 or NewHorizonsCoreMod, where calling its API or a mixin is not enough. The switch, when needed, gets its own design record naming the integration that requires it, and updates `LICENSE`, the jar's license, [0001](0001-port-policy-and-licensing.md) and [CONTRIBUTING](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). Until then, GPL code is not copied in; calling GPL mods' public APIs, reading their constants and mixing into them is integration and stays allowed | Owner: tighter integration with the pack and RWG is worth GPL if it comes to that, but not before it is needed. MIT upstream code and our own code can move to GPL-3.0 at any time; the reverse is not possible, so the move waits |
| D24 | 2026-11-01 | Coexistence | Lost Cities generation gets out of the way of other world generation, in a way that isn't jarring and has no sharp cutoffs. Where another mod's structure (a village, a Roguelike tower, a temple, a house) would land in a city, the city builds around it so it becomes a feature of the city, instead of overwriting it or being cut by it. Nearby villages are sewn into cities: paths lead from the city into the village, and the city never writes over village chunks. How each generator is predicted and given room is settled by a design record from the coexistence research | Owner's rule of thumb: cities belong in a shared landscape. It extends D21's avoid-and-guard treatments into building around other structures |
| D25 | 2026-11-01 | Blocks | Variety is the goal for building blocks, not a stand-in per missing block. Every decorative and construction block GTNH 2.9.0 offers (Chisel, ChiselTones, Ztones, GT stones and rock wool, Railcraft stones, Et Futurum, Botania, Thaumcraft, wood mods, glass and lamp mods and the rest) is catalogued with a contact sheet and judged against upstream's look. Palettes then draw weighted variety from the fitting blocks for each role (wall, trim, floor, glass, roof, light). The D19 mapping stays as the baseline and the fallback when a mod is absent. Varying building geometry is the other half of the problem and comes later ([ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md)) | Owner: a common complaint about Lost Cities is that cities feel synthetic because buildings aren't unique enough; a richer re-skin fixes half of that |
| D26 | 2026-11-01 | Coexistence | [0006](0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) is accepted: pure structure oracles per mod, landmark cores the city never writes in, edge rings of parks and plazas, soft-edge strips instead of border walls, and roads that detour or end in plazas. Villages inside a city's radius become its old town, ringed by lower building density. Lanes may cross village chunks at the surface to meet the village's own paths, never within 2 blocks of a village building. AE2 meteorites that land in a city get a crater basin the city steps down toward. The Roguelike Dungeons steering mixin is built in M08 rather than waiting for alpha | Owner's choices; one connected town around villages, and no towers on streets |
