| D23 | 2026-11-01 | Licensing | The mod stays MIT by default. | D24 | 2026-11-01 | Coexistence | Lost Cities generation gets out of the way of other world generation, in a way that isn't jarring and has no sharp cutoffs. Nearby villages are sewn into cities: paths lead from the city into the village, and the city never writes over village chunks. How each generator is predicted and given room is settled by a design record from the coexistence research | Owner's rule of thumb: cities belong in a shared landscape.
