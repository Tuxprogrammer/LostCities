# 07 · Scattered structures

How Lost Cities: New Horizons places upstream's scattered structures (cabins, radio towers, oil rigs, and the gas stations and restaurants beside highways) and its decorative "stuff" columns, and what replaces upstream's structure avoidance. Upstream's behavior is in [original-spec/07-scattered](../../original-spec/07-scattered/index.md); the asset formats are in [05-assets](../05-assets/index.md#scattered-stuff-and-predefined-formats).

Status: *Accepted* where a row cites a decision record or a decision-log row; *Proposed* where it says so, with the recommendation in [Proposed, not decided](#proposed-not-decided). Bug IDs are from [BUGS.md](../../original-spec/BUGS.md); a row that names one fixes it, with a regression test ([0001](../../design/0001-port-policy-and-licensing.md) §2). Facts about other mods are *verified* with a pinned permalink or marked *unverified* ([new-spec README](../README.md#how-to-read)).

## Summary

- **Scattered structures are faithful**: upstream's areas, chances, weights, placement and terrain fitting, with the five bundled structures, in the countryside between cities (M07, [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md)).
- **They share the overworld with other mods' structures.** Upstream's structure avoidance is not ported. The pure structure oracles of [0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) tell the layout where villages, Roguelike towers, Witchery and LootGames sites, AE2 meteorites and RWG2's landmarks will be, and scattered structures stay out of their landmark cores and edge rings ([0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) Consequences, M07).
- **Heights come from the height oracle**, biomes from RWG's biome provider; the plan never reads a loaded chunk ([0005](../../design/0005-rwg-overlay.md#4-height-oracle) §4).
- **Stuff columns** (chains and cobwebs in buildings) keep upstream's rules, with Et Futurum's chain for `minecraft:chain` (D19) and a seeded, order-independent random.
- **All 23 upstream bugs of this area are fixed or cannot occur.** The bundled assets hide several of them: every bundled scattered building has one floor with one unconditional part, so the floor and overlay fixes (SCAT-10, SCAT-11, SCAT-13) change nothing they generate.

## Proposed, not decided

### P07-1 Selection biome

Upstream filters the whole candidate list by the biome of the area's corner chunk (SCAT-07), so a structure that fits most of the area is excluded when the corner differs, and one kept on the corner's biome then fails on its real footprint. **Recommendation:** keep a list entry when its biome filter accepts the biome of at least one chunk of the area, read from RWG's biome provider at each chunk's center (64 lookups for an 8-chunk area, once per plan). Selection and placement then run as upstream, with the same draws; the footprint's terrain stats still test each chunk's biome, so a structure never lands on the wrong biome.

### P07-2 Deep ocean for the oil rig

The oil rig requires `#minecraft:is_deep_ocean`. Neither RWG reports a deep-ocean biome: their oceans are six base biomes registered as `OCEAN` and `BEACH` ([05 P05-6](../05-assets/index.md#p05-6-biome-vocabulary), *verified* there). RWG2's ocean floor is about Y 52 in shallow and Y 34 in deep ocean, and classic RWG's test ocean about Y 45 ([rwg.md](../00-pack-integration/rwg.md)). **Recommendation:** "deep ocean" means `type:OCEAN` with the height oracle's floor at least 15 blocks below sea level (Y 48 or lower) at every footprint chunk's center. Oil rigs then stand where RWG2 has deep water and on classic RWG's open ocean, as upstream's stand on deep ocean.

### P07-3 Landmark rings outside cities

Edge rings are city chunks around a core ([0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) §1), so outside a city a core has no ring, and a cabin could be placed in the chunk next to a village or a Roguelike lot. **Recommendation:** scattered footprints also stay out of a core's eight-neighbor chunks outside cities. The oracle answer is already at hand, the cost is nothing, and a scattered building never abuts another mod's structure.

### P07-4 Population denial under scattered structures

The T2 denial of `LAKE`, `LAVA`, `DUNGEON` and NewHorizonsCoreMod's oil (`CUSTOM`) applies to population windows that touch a city chunk ([other-worldgen.md](../00-pack-integration/other-worldgen.md)). A lake or an oil fountain can still cut into a cabin or a gas station. **Recommendation:** deny the same events when the window touches a scattered footprint chunk, with the same handler: scattered buildings are meant to be moved into as much as city buildings ([0004](../../design/0004-gtnh-layer-and-tier-ceiling.md) §5).

### P07-5 Roguelike towers and scattered structures

The Roguelike Dungeons steering mixin rejects candidate points inside city chunks and edge rings, unless the point is that dungeon's predicted lot ([0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) §5). A tower whose predicted lot fails can still land on a scattered structure. **Recommendation:** the mixin also rejects points inside a scattered footprint. Plans are pure, so the test is as cheap as the city test, and it is built with the mixin in M08.

### P07-6 Floors in scattered structures

Loot and spawner conditions count floors from a city ground level ([08](../08-loot-mobs-blocks/index.md#conditions-and-selection)). Upstream reuses the `BuildingInfo` of the chunk, which outside a city has no city of its own, so the floor a cabin chest reports is not meaningful. **Recommendation:** count a scattered structure's floors from its plan's lowest level, with floor division by 6, no cellars, and the structure's own floor count. The bundled chests then sit on floors 0 to 3 and draw the ordinary bands, as their upstream counterparts drew mostly ordinary vanilla tables.
