# 06 · Highways and railways

The highways that link cities and the railways, stations and rail dungeons that run under and between them, over RWG terrain. Upstream's default intercity highway planner and its railway grid ([06-highways-railways](../../original-spec/06-highways-railways/index.md)) carry over; the legacy noise highways, the planner's thread pool and its saved hub data do not (D11), and monorails wait with the sphere landscapes (D12). Heights come from the RWG height oracle and biomes from RWG's biome provider ([0005](../../design/0005-rwg-overlay.md)); landmark cores are obstacles highways route around or end before, deep railways pass under them, and shallow railways dip under them and climb back ([0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md#consequences), M06, D30, D31); highways and primary roads are where speed blocks are walked on (D28 rule 3). A highway reaches each city and each dead end at the ground's own level by ramps and ends on a plaza, never cut (alpha review AR-10, [0010](../../design/0010-highway-and-railway-networks.md)). The cities' subways (rail corridors under their streets, `04-city-layout`) are switched off with the `railways` module.

Status: *Accepted*. Every row links a design record or decision-log row; the items this area once left *Proposed* (P06-1 to P06-8) were settled by the owner's answers in D31 and are recorded, with their ID, in the rows they belong to. Parts are written by the terrain phase ([03-terrain](../03-terrain/pipeline-on-rwg.md#stage-map)); the planners are pure Java in `layout` (D6).
