# Other overworld world generation

Cities share the GTNH 2.9.0 overworld with roughly twenty other world generators. Most are harmless to a city: they place small decoration on grass and dirt, or work deep underground. A handful can damage a city or be damaged by one: villages, Roguelike Dungeons, the NewHorizonsCoreMod oil fountains, AE2 meteorites, LootGames puzzle dungeons, Witchery structures, mineshafts, vanilla dungeons, and RWG2's own landmarks (volcanoes, lava caves, river-junction pillars). GT ore veins are covered in [ore-veins.md](ore-veins.md), and RWG terrain and the overlay hook in [rwg.md](rwg.md).

Status: *Accepted* (spike S3; [decision log](../../design/decision-log.md) D21, with record [0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) and D26 for other mods' surface structures). Settles open question Q8. How each structure's site is predicted and given room is researched in [coexistence.md](coexistence.md).

## Answer to Q8

The inventory and the hooks are read from source at the pack's tags and from the pack's shipped `config/`. The risk ratings are checked against real overlaps in the alpha screenshots.

Four treatments. Each pack feature gets one ([inventory](#inventory)).

1. **Build around in the layout (T1).** Where another generator's site can be predicted from the world seed, coordinates, the biome provider and the height oracle, a pure structure oracle tells the layout, and the city builds around it ([0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md)). The structure's chunks become a landmark core the city never writes in, ringed by parks, plazas and soft edges; villages are sewn in with lanes, and inside a city's radius become its old town; an AE2 meteorite gets a crater basin. Applies to VillageNames villages, Roguelike Dungeons (a predicted lot), Witchery, LootGames dungeons, AE2 meteorites, and RWG2 volcanoes, lava caves and river junctions (terrain landmarks). The layout stays deterministic and never loads a chunk to decide. Upstream's avoidance (`avoidVillages`) remains as the `AVOID` option.
2. **Deny the population event in city chunks (T2).** Forge's `PopulateChunkEvent.Populate` can be denied per chunk and per event type; RWG2 already does this to keep Railcraft geodes off its ocean floors. A Lost Cities handler denies, for chunks whose population window touches a city: vanilla dungeons (`DUNGEON`), surface and underground lakes (`LAKE`, `LAVA`), the NewHorizonsCoreMod oil spheres and fountains (`CUSTOM`), and the Railcraft quarry and geode populators. Cities keep their own spawners and loot, so no city room becomes a mossy dungeon by accident.
3. **Coexist (T3).** Underground and small surface features stay as they are: strongholds, mineshafts, Et Futurum geodes and fossils, flowers, fruit trees, hives, Thaumcraft structures and nodes, and slime islands. Faithful to upstream, which lets vanilla structures run under its cities.
4. **Steering mixin (T4).** Roguelike Dungeons picks its tower site by reading the world, so its predicted lot can miss. An optional compat mixin on `Dungeon.validLocation`, built in M08, rejects any point inside a city chunk or edge ring other than that dungeon's predicted lot, so the tower lands in its lot, outside the city, or not at all ([0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) §5). RWG2's own mixin into Witchery is the precedent. Witchery needs no mixin: its site is deterministic. A guard for any other generator follows only if the alpha screenshots show its structure in a city.

RWG's own decoration (trees, boulders, small ruins) is not a treatment on this page: the city clears it inside its footprint and keeps it outside ([0005](../../design/0005-rwg-overlay.md) §3a).

A post-population restore pass ([ore-veins.md](ore-veins.md), option B) is not used for any feature on this page: no restore pass rewrites another mod's blocks ([0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) §5).

## Inventory

Every mod in the pack with an overworld world-generation hook, found by searching every jar for `IWorldGenerator`, `PopulateChunkEvent`, `DecorateBiomeEvent` and `InitMapGenEvent`, then checked against the pack's config. Risk is for a city chunk: **high** (damages buildings or makes them unlivable), **medium** (visible, rarely harmful), **low** (cosmetic or deep underground), **none** (does not run in the overworld as configured).

## Treatments in detail

| ID | Mechanism | Hook | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Build around | The layout's landmark lots: core, edge ring and, for villages, transition rings and lanes ([0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) §1 to §4) | Inputs come from pure per-mod structure oracles under `compat.<mod>`: VillageNames, Roguelike Dungeons, Witchery, LootGames, AE2 and RWG2's chunk-manager predicates (`getVolcanoVicinityCoordinates`, `getLavaCaveCoordinates`, `getRiverJunctionStrength`). Each reads only the world seed, coordinates, the biome provider, the height oracle and the other mod's config; no chunk is loaded and `world.rand` is never reseeded. The layout package stays free of Minecraft types: the glue passes one aggregate `StructureOracle` in. Upstream's avoidance keys become `villageTreatment` and `landmarkTreatment` (`BUILD_AROUND` by default, `AVOID`, `IGNORE`). |
| T2 | Population denial | `PopulateChunkEvent.Populate` on `MinecraftForge.TERRAIN_GEN_BUS`, set `Result.DENY` | Deny only these types: `DUNGEON`, `LAKE`, `LAVA`, `CUSTOM`, `RAILCRAFT_QUARRY` (compared by name, as RWG2 does). Deny when any of the four chunks the population window covers is a city chunk. `CUSTOM` is shared: in the pack, NewHorizonsCoreMod's oil, Galacticraft's oil (factor 0) and BuildCraft's springs (off) use it, so denying it removes only oil and springs (*verified* by a search of every pack jar for classes referencing the `CUSTOM` populate type). |
| T3 | Coexist | None | Nothing to build. Underground structures may cut subways and basements, as in upstream. |
| T4 | Steering mixin | `Dungeon.validLocation` (Roguelike Dungeons) | Built in M08. Optional, under `compat`, guarded by `Loader.isModLoaded`, with a comment saying why no event works ([CONTRIBUTING § Mixins](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#gtnh-integration)). Rejects a point inside a city chunk or edge ring unless it is that dungeon's predicted lot ([0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) §5). A guard for another generator only if the alpha screenshots show its structure in a city. |

Upstream's avoidance, which T1 keeps as the `AVOID` option: [StructureAvoidance.java](https://github.com/McJtyMods/LostCities/blob/f18a094d/src/main/java/mcjty/lostcities/worldgen/lost/StructureAvoidance.java), [Config.java#L40-L47](https://github.com/McJtyMods/LostCities/blob/f18a094d/src/main/java/mcjty/lostcities/setup/Config.java#L40-L47) (`DEF_AVOID_STRUCTURES`: mansions, jungle and desert pyramids, igloos, swamp huts, pillager outposts; in this pack only igloos exist, and they do not generate on RWG).

## Decisions

Recorded in the [decision log](../../design/decision-log.md) as D21, with D24, D26 and record [0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md) for other mods' surface structures:

- **Treatments** T1 to T4 apply as listed in the [inventory](#inventory).
- **Villages** are built around and sewn in. A village inside a city's radius becomes its old town, ringed by two lower-density rings, with lanes from city streets into the village paths. Lanes may cross village chunks at the surface to meet those paths, never within 2 blocks of a village piece.
- **AE2 meteorites** that land in a city get a crater basin the city steps down toward.
- **Roguelike Dungeons** get a predicted lot, and the steering mixin (T4) is built in M08 rather than waiting for the alpha screenshots.
- **Witchery, LootGames and RWG2's landmarks** are built around; none needs a mixin.
- **Thaumcraft** coexists (T3) and is watched at alpha.

- Whether Thaumcraft mounds land in city parks or spill onto streets.
