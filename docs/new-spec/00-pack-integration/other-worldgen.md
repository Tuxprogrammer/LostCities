# Other overworld world generation

Cities share the GTNH 2.9.0 overworld with roughly twenty other world generators. Most are harmless to a city: they place small decoration on grass and dirt, or work deep underground. A handful can damage a city or be damaged by one: villages, Roguelike Dungeons, the NewHorizonsCoreMod oil fountains, AE2 meteorites, LootGames puzzle dungeons, Witchery structures, mineshafts, vanilla dungeons, and RWG2's own landmarks (volcanoes, lava caves, river-junction pillars). GT ore veins are covered in [ore-veins.md](ore-veins.md), and RWG terrain and the overlay hook in [rwg.md](rwg.md).

Status: *Proposed* (spike S3). Settles open question Q8 once the owner accepts it.

## Proposed answer to Q8

Confidence: **high** for the inventory and the hooks (read from source at the pack's tags and from the pack's shipped `config/`), **medium** for the risk ratings until the alpha screenshots show real overlaps.

Four treatments, cheapest first. Each pack feature gets one ([inventory](#inventory)).

1. **Avoid in the layout (T1).** Where another generator's site is a pure function of the world seed, coordinates and biome, the city layout asks for it and keeps those chunks free of buildings, as upstream already does for villages (`avoidVillages`, default on). Applies to VillageNames villages, RWG2 volcanoes, lava caves and river junctions, and (owner's choice) AE2 meteorites and LootGames dungeons. The layout stays deterministic and never loads a chunk to decide.
2. **Deny the population event in city chunks (T2).** Forge's `PopulateChunkEvent.Populate` can be denied per chunk and per event type; RWG2 already does this to keep Railcraft geodes off its ocean floors. A Lost Cities handler denies, for chunks whose population window touches a city: vanilla dungeons (`DUNGEON`), surface and underground lakes (`LAKE`, `LAVA`), the NewHorizonsCoreMod oil spheres and fountains (`CUSTOM`), and the Railcraft quarry and geode populators. Cities keep their own spawners and loot, so no city room becomes a mossy dungeon by accident.
3. **Coexist (T3).** Underground and small surface features stay as they are: strongholds, mineshafts, Et Futurum geodes and fossils, flowers, fruit trees, hives, Thaumcraft nodes, slime islands. Faithful to upstream, which lets vanilla structures run under its cities.
4. **Mod-specific guard (T4), only where nothing else works.** Roguelike Dungeons and Witchery place large surface structures from `IWorldGenerator`s with no event to deny. A small optional compat mixin rejects their placement inside a city footprint, on the precedent of RWG2's own mixin into Witchery. These wait for the alpha screenshots: if no dungeon tower or witch shack is seen in a city, they are not built.

A post-population restore pass ([ore-veins.md](ore-veins.md), option B) is the backstop for anything that still lands in a city; it is not needed for any feature on this page.

## Treatments in detail

| ID | Mechanism | Hook | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Layout avoidance | The layout's city-chunk decision, as upstream's `StructureAvoidance` | Inputs are pure functions: VillageNames' start test, RWG2's chunk-manager predicates (`getVolcanoVicinityCoordinates`, `getLavaCaveCoordinates`, `getRiverJunctionStrength`), and optionally the AE2 and LootGames grids. No chunk is loaded. The layout package stays free of Minecraft types: the glue passes a `StructureSites` interface in. Upstream's options (`avoidVillages`, `avoidVillagesAdjacent`, `avoidStructures`) carry over as the switches. |
| T2 | Population denial | `PopulateChunkEvent.Populate` on `MinecraftForge.TERRAIN_GEN_BUS`, set `Result.DENY` | Deny only these types: `DUNGEON`, `LAKE`, `LAVA`, `CUSTOM`, `RAILCRAFT_QUARRY` (compared by name, as RWG2 does). Deny when any of the four chunks the population window covers is a city chunk. `CUSTOM` is shared: in the pack, NewHorizonsCoreMod's oil, Galacticraft's oil (factor 0) and BuildCraft's springs (off) use it, so denying it removes only oil and springs (*verified* by a search of every pack jar for classes referencing the `CUSTOM` populate type). |
| T3 | Coexist | None | Nothing to build. Underground structures may cut subways and basements, as in upstream. |
| T4 | Compat mixin | `Dungeon.validLocation` (Roguelike), `WitcheryComponent.calcGroundHeight` (Witchery) | Optional, under `compat`, guarded by `Loader.isModLoaded`, with a comment saying why no event works ([CONTRIBUTING § Mixins](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#gtnh-integration)). Built only if the alpha screenshots show the conflict. |

Upstream reference for T1: [StructureAvoidance.java](https://github.com/McJtyMods/LostCities/blob/f18a094d/src/main/java/mcjty/lostcities/worldgen/lost/StructureAvoidance.java), [Config.java#L40-L47](https://github.com/McJtyMods/LostCities/blob/f18a094d/src/main/java/mcjty/lostcities/setup/Config.java#L40-L47) (`DEF_AVOID_STRUCTURES`: mansions, jungle and desert pyramids, igloos, swamp huts, pillager outposts; in this pack only igloos exist, and they are rare).

## Questions for the owner

| Question | Recommendation | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Should cities avoid villages, as upstream does by default? | Yes (T1, `avoidVillages` on). | Faithful and simple; costs city area near the roughly one village per 50×50 chunks. The alternative, villages inside cities, gives overlapping houses and paths. |
| AE2 meteorites in cities: crater or empty lot? | Crater (T3). | A ruined-city look and no coupling to AE2's grid formula, against one building per crater being unlivable. T1 needs AE2's seeding formula replicated or called. |
| Roguelike Dungeons in cities: accept, mixin, or ask GTNH for a hook? | Wait for the alpha screenshots; if towers appear in streets, a compat mixin (T4). | A mixin into a GTNH-maintained mod works now but must be re-checked on every bump; an upstream hook is cleaner but depends on another team ([pack-contribution-policy]). |
