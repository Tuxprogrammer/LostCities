# 03 · Terrain

How Lost Cities: New Horizons turns a finished RWG chunk into a city chunk or a blended neighbor. Upstream 1.20 rewrites vanilla noise terrain from a world-generation feature ([03-terrain](../../original-spec/03-terrain/index.md)). Here the same work runs as an overlay on RWG2 Continents and classic RWG terrain, through a delegating chunk provider with a terrain phase and a population phase, inside the 1.7.10 height range of Y 0 to 255 ([0005](../../design/0005-rwg-overlay.md)). The city also writes nothing in the landmark cores other mods' structures occupy, and meets them with soft edges instead of walls ([0006](../../design/0006-build-around-other-worldgen.md)).

Status: *Accepted*. Every row links a design record or decision-log row; the items this area once left *Proposed* (P03-1 to P03-14) were settled by the owner's answers in D31 and are recorded, with their ID, in the rows and sheets they belong to. Facts about other mods are *verified* with a permalink at the pack's tag, or *vanilla* (read from the 1.7.10 server with Forge 10.13.4.1614 applied, which has no public pinned source, as in [rwg.md](../00-pack-integration/rwg.md)), or *unverified*.

## How to read the table

- **Original** links the upstream sheet, or names the system or tunable inside it.
- **Here** is one of: *same* (ported as upstream behaves, apart from the fixes in the last column), *changed*, *dropped* (not ported), *deferred* (not ported before beta; the spec keeps the upstream behavior on file), or *new* (no upstream counterpart).
- **Bugs** lists the upstream bug IDs from [BUGS.md](../../original-spec/BUGS.md) the row must not reproduce. IDs in parentheses are the same bug under another area's ID. Every `TERR-*` ID appears in at least one row.
