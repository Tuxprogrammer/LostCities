# Highway part placement

What gets written into a chunk that carries a highway: which part (tunnel, open road, bridge) of which shape (straight, crossing, bend, T), at which height, the clearing above it, its supports, and the railing openings left for highway-side scattered buildings (gas stations, restaurants).

| # | Field | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Identity** | `worldgen/gen/Highways` (`generateHighways`, `generateHighwayPart`, `getJunction`, supports, railing clearing), asset data `worldgen/lost/regassets/data/HighwayParts` (the world style's `parts.highways` block). Reads world-style fields `highwaysupport`, `highwaysupportpart`, the part meta key `support`, profile option `highwaySupports`, and `Scattered.getHighwayRailingOpenings` (`07-scattered`). |
| 2 | **Inputs** | The chunk's `BuildingInfo` (ground level, city flag, city level, water level, profile, palette, heightmap) and its east, south and north neighbors' `BuildingInfo`; the chunk's `HighwayInfo` ([highway-occupancy.md](highway-occupancy.md)); the world style's part selector and support settings; the chunk-generation random. |
| 3 | **Numbers** | See [Numbers](#numbers). |
| 4 | **Behavior** | See [Behavior](#behavior). |
| 5 | **Determinism** | One random draw per part list with more than one entry, from the chunk's generation random. See [Determinism](#determinism). |
| 6 | **Data formats** | `HighwayParts` JSON; world-style support fields. See [Data formats](#data-formats). |
| 7 | **Output** | Blocks of the chosen part(s), cleared air above, support columns or support parts below, removed iron bars at railing openings. |
| 8 | **Relationships** | Called from `LostCityTerrainFeature` for normal and city chunks; parts are generated by `LostCityTerrainFeature.generatePart` (`04-city-layout`, `05-assets`); railways are written afterwards and can overwrite highways ([railway-parts.md](railway-parts.md)). |
| 9 | **1.20 dependencies** | `BlockTags.LEAVES`/`LOGS`, `Blocks.IRON_BARS`, `BlockState` palettes, support parts that run down to the world's minimum build height (-64). |
| 10 | **Bugs** | `HWY-02`, `HWY-12`. |

## Numbers

| Item | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Highway ground Y | `groundLevel + level × 6` | `groundLevel` = profile `GROUNDLEVEL` (default 71; the outside profile's outside city spheres) |
| Tunnel test, city chunk | `cityLevel > level` | |
| Tunnel test, non-city chunk | heightmap height `> groundLevel + level × 6 + 3` | 3-block threshold |
| Clearing above open and bridge parts | 15 blocks, from the part's top | Skipped in cavern profiles; leaves and logs are kept |
| Support column length | at most 40 blocks | Down from highway ground Y − 1 while the block is air, water or lava |
| Support column positions (part-local, before rotation) | (0, 15), (0, 0); also (15, 0) for bend and T parts | Only on the part's west edge plus one corner |
| Support part | repeated downward from highway ground Y − 1 to the world's minimum build height while blocks are empty | `gen/Supports.generatePart` (`04-city-layout`) |
| Railing-opening clear | Y from highway ground + 1 up to (not including) ground + part height | Only iron bars are removed, along one 16-block chunk edge |
| `highwaySupports` | default true | `floating`, `space`, `void_outside` profiles set false |

Bundled highway parts (all 16 × 16 chunks; `05-assets` owns the catalog):

| Shape | Tunnel | Open (in city) | Bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Straight | `highway_tunnel` (6 high) | `highway_open` (12) | `highway_bridge` (9) |
| Crossing | `highway_tunnel_bi` (6) | `highway_open_bi` (12) | `highway_bridge_bi` (9) |
| Bend | `highway_tunnel_bend` (6) | `highway_open_bend` (12) | `highway_bridge_bend` (9) |
| T junction | `highway_tunnel_t` (6) | `highway_open_t` (12) | `highway_bridge_t` (9) |

None of them carries a `support` meta entry; the bundled world styles `standard` and `standard_everywhere` set `highwaysupport` to `v`. The parts `highway_gas_station`, `highway_restaurant` and `highway_restaurant_parking` belong to scattered buildings (`07-scattered`).

There are no ramp parts and no ramp logic: a route keeps one level from end to end, and where it meets a city at another level it is a tunnel, open road or bridge at its own level.

## Behavior

### When it runs (server)

1. (server) Normal (non-city) chunk: after terrain correction, the `PostGenOutsideChunkEvent`, and bridges; before scattered structures. `generateHighways` always runs and does nothing when there is no highway.
2. (server) City chunk: after the building or street, ruins, and street decorations (which are skipped on highway chunks); only when either highway level is set; before rubble.
3. (server) Railways, rail dungeons, torch fixing and explosion damage run after both, so railway parts can overwrite highway blocks.

### Choosing parts (server)

1. (server) Read the chunk's `HighwayInfo`.
2. (server) **Same level on both axes** (a crossing of two routes, a bend, or a T): build the set of connected sides from the route hits whose route level equals this level. For each segment containing the chunk: an X segment adds west if the chunk is east of the segment's west end and east if it is west of the east end; a Z segment adds north and south the same way.
   - Two sides that are not opposite → **bend**. The unrotated bend joins west and south; west+south → no rotation, north+west → 90°, north+east → 180°, otherwise (east+south) → 270°.
   - Three sides → **T**. The unrotated T joins west, east and south; the missing side picks the rotation: north missing → none, east missing → 90°, south missing → 180°, west missing → 270°.
   - Anything else (four sides, or no route hits, which is always the case in legacy mode) → **crossing**, unrotated.
   - The in-city test (step 4) uses the east and south neighbors.
3. (server) **Different levels on the two axes**: generate the lower one first, because each part clears the space above it. Each is a straight part.
4. (server) **One axis only**: one straight part. X highway: unrotated, in-city test against the north and south neighbors. Z highway: rotated 90°, in-city test against the east neighbor twice (the west neighbor is never checked; `HWY-02`).

### Placing one part (server)

1. (server) Highway ground Y = `groundLevel + level × 6`.
2. (server) If the chunk needs a tunnel at this level (see Numbers), pick from the tunnel list for the shape and generate it at that Y with hard-air mode `WATERLEVEL`. No clearing above.
3. (server) Else if the chunk is a city chunk, the two neighbors are city chunks, and the level is at or below both neighbors' city levels, pick from the open list. Else pick from the bridge list. Generate it at that Y with `WATERLEVEL`. Then, unless the profile is a cavern, clear every column from the part's top for 15 blocks: replace everything except leaves and logs with air; in a drowned city (water level above ground level) fill with water up to the water level first.
4. (server) Picking from a list: a one-entry list gives that entry; otherwise the chunk's generation random picks an index.
5. (server) Supports, only when `highwaySupports` is true:
   - Support character: the part's `support` meta character; if the part has none, the world style's `highwaysupport` character and `highwaysupportpart` name.
   - If a support part name is set, generate that part downward from highway ground Y − 1 (rotated with the highway) until each column hits a non-empty block or the world floor.
   - Else, if a support character is set, look it up in the chunk's compiled palette (a missing entry throws `RuntimeException`, `HWY-12`) and drop columns at the positions in Numbers, rotated with the highway, each at most 40 blocks.
   - Supports are attempted for tunnels and open roads too; there the ground is usually solid, so nothing is placed.
6. (server) Railing openings, only when the world style has scattered settings: ask `Scattered` which of the four sides border a highway-attached scattered building planned at exactly this highway ground Y and facing this chunk. For each such side, remove iron bars along that chunk edge between highway ground + 1 and ground + the part's height.

### Gas stations and restaurants (cross-area hook)

Highway-side scattered buildings are planned by `07-scattered`: a scattered building whose reference is "near highway" searches its area for placements whose connecting edge runs along highway chunks of one common ground height that are not tunnels, and stores that height. This sheet only covers the railing openings the highway leaves for them (step 6). `Scattered.avoidScattered` keeps ordinary scattered buildings off highway chunks.

## Determinism

- **Random:** `LostCityTerrainFeature.getRandomPart` draws `nextInt(size)` from the chunk's generation random (`GenerationContext`, a `LegacyRandomSource` seeded from a mix of the world seed, the dimension ID's `hashCode`, and the chunk coordinates). The draw happens only for lists with more than one entry. The bundled lists all have one entry, so the bundled highways consume no randomness. With custom lists, the pick depends on how many draws the chunk made before (building, street, ruins), so it is stable for a given code version but shifts if earlier generation changes.
- **Neighbors:** the in-city test reads neighbor `BuildingInfo` (planning data, not chunks), so it does not depend on generation order and does not load chunks.
- **Writes:** only into the chunk being generated.

## Data formats

`HighwayParts` (world style `parts.highways`; field format owned by `05-assets`). Each field takes a part name or a list of part names; a missing field defaults to the one-element list shown.

| Field | Default | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `tunnel` | `highway_tunnel` | Straight tunnel |
| `open` | `highway_open` | Straight, in city |
| `bridge` | `highway_bridge` | Straight, elsewhere |
| `tunnel_bi` | `highway_tunnel_bi` | Crossing tunnel |
| `open_bi` | `highway_open_bi` | Crossing in city |
| `bridge_bi` | `highway_bridge_bi` | Crossing elsewhere |
| `tunnel_bend` | `highway_tunnel_bend` | Bend tunnel |
| `open_bend` | `highway_open_bend` | Bend in city |
| `bridge_bend` | `highway_bridge_bend` | Bend elsewhere |
| `tunnel_t` | `highway_tunnel_t` | T tunnel |
| `open_t` | `highway_open_t` | T in city |
| `bridge_t` | `highway_bridge_t` | T elsewhere |

The record's helpers `tunnel(bi)`, `open(bi)` and `bridge(bi)` have no caller (*unused*). A part selector equal to the default is not written back (`get()` returns empty).

World style: `highwaysupport` (one palette character, optional), `highwaysupportpart` (part name, optional). Part meta: `support` (character). Profile: `highwaySupports` (boolean).

## Output

The highway part blocks for each level present in the chunk; air (or water) above open and bridge parts; support blocks or support-part blocks below; removed iron bars at railing openings. Highways end at hub chunks with a straight piece; there is no end cap (*inferred* from the code paths).

## Relationships

- Called by `LostCityTerrainFeature.doNormalChunk` and `doCityChunk` (`03-terrain`, `04-city-layout`).
- Uses `LostCityTerrainFeature.generatePart`, `clearRange`, `getRandomPart` (`04-city-layout`), `gen/Supports` (`04-city-layout`), `Scattered.getHighwayRailingOpenings` (`07-scattered`), `BuildingInfo.isTunnel` (`04-city-layout`, logic restated above).
- Part assets and palettes: `05-assets`.

## 1.20 dependencies

| Mechanism | Where | Why it matters for 1.7.10 |
|---|---|---|
| `BlockTags.LEAVES` and `BlockTags.LOGS` | `isClearableAboveHighway` | 1.7.10 has no block tags; leaves and logs are found with `Block.isLeaves`/`isWood` or materials. |
| `Blocks.IRON_BARS` by identity | `clearRailing` | Exists in 1.7.10 as `Blocks.iron_bars`; a palette mapped to another bar block would not be cleared. |
| World minimum build height (-64) | support parts via `Supports.generatePart` | 1.7.10's floor is Y 0. |
| Level 32 network highways | `highwayNetworkLevel` up to 32 | Y 71 + 192 = 263, plus up to 12 blocks of part, is above 1.7.10's 255 ceiling. |
| `BlockState` palettes and identity | throughout | 1.7.10 uses block plus metadata. |

## On 1.12

`LostCitiesTerrainGenerator.generateHighways` on `upstream/1.12` has only straight and crossing parts (`highway_<kind>` and `highway_<kind>_bi`, no bend or T, no part lists), generates the multi-level pair by "level X is 0 first", reads the support only from the part's `support` meta (no world-style fallback, no support parts), places two columns, and has no railing openings. The Z highway's in-city test uses the east neighbor twice there too.

## Bugs

- `HWY-02`: the in-city test for Z highways checks the east neighbor twice and never the west neighbor (also on 1.12).
- `HWY-12`: a support character missing from the palette throws `RuntimeException` during generation.
